# R1 and packet mechanism status

The historical R1 trial uses human-labeled QASPER and ContractNLI development data, equal 1,100 BGE tokenizer source-token budgets, fixed legacy leaders plus grouping, and a separately re-embedded structural index. The alternate index is real within that isolated trial; it did not modify production. On QASPER, complete evidence was 53.40% current chunks, 56.31% grouping, 52.91% re-embedded. On ContractNLI it was 69.06%, 72.64%, 74.59%. Top-1 overlap fell under grouping in both datasets. The two datasets therefore do not justify a universal index promotion. The R1 report is preserved on the separate local branch at `fix/evidence-v3-series-r1:backend/eval/reports/evidence-v3-r1-broad-2026-09-26.md`.

The historical corrected packet selector compared the same current chunks and equal source-context caps. Dense QASPER was 53.40% with or without packets; TEI QASPER fell from 50.97% to 50.49% (one lost evidence span). ContractNLI dense and TEI complete-evidence rates were unchanged at 69.06% and 77.04%. Counterevidence added no complete-evidence gain. These are negative/null observations and remain published. See [packet report](../../backend/eval/reports/evidence-v3-packets-2026-09-26.md).

This hardening pass extracted shared source-interval and scoring helpers so packet evaluation no longer imports optional R1 trials. Frozen 176-case and descriptive 416-case historical outcomes were identical after offline rescoring.

A separate, newly frozen public transfer trial used the original human-reviewed extractive spans from [RAG_Multilingual](https://huggingface.co/datasets/projecte-aina/RAG_Multilingual) at revision `a7020cfffaeff43c23f2847da0b45df51a49faf3` (CC-BY-SA-4.0), with eight Spanish and eight English validation examples and twelve of each in its disjoint test split. The source files and selected document/distractor IDs were hashed before scoring in ignored `rag-transfer-v2/manifest.json` (SHA-256 `b7e2cf2293cb864c195db772d1e73eb48ae7bb80f08f4c80722da106dec9bb92`). All four routes used the same 512 BGE tokenizer source-token cap and BGE reranker served by local TEI. Structural chunks were independently built and reranked; packet neighbors and counterevidence could only come from the selected source family. The expanded model-written answers were unused. This is a public transfer set, not enterprise data or QASPER relabeled as a new locked test.

All routes reached 16/16 complete evidence and top-1 evidence in validation and 24/24 of each in test. Flat BGE, flat packets, and packets with counterevidence tied exactly; the structural route also tied on evidence and averaged 408.5 rendered tokens versus 438.8 for flat on test. The perfect flat baseline is a ceiling effect and this small, single-span sample cannot establish a representative deployment gain. The outcome is null for packet quality and inconclusive for R1 quality, so both remain optional and off. The ignored `rag-transfer-v2/result.json` retains every case and the exact failed-to-improve comparisons; its run exited zero. The trial runner is preserved at `fix/evidence-v3-hardening:backend/eval/rag_transfer_trial.py`, outside the core series. A more demanding frozen stress set and independently labeled enterprise-like claim/evidence families remain necessary, as does full-prompt generation cost and answer-quality measurement.
